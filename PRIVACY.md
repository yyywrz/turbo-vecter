# Privacy Policy

**Turbo‑Vecter** is a local‑only browser extension that adds cursor visual effects.

## Data Collection
- Turbo‑Vecter does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal data.
- No analytics, tracking, or remote logging is performed.

## Data Storage
- The extension stores only your selected settings (effect and language) in extension storage (`chrome.storage`).
- This data stays in your browser and is used solely to remember your preferences.

## Permissions
- `storage`: to save your preferences.
- `host_permissions` on `<all_urls>`: to run the visual effects on pages you visit.

## Third‑Party Services
- None.

## Changes
If this policy changes, it will be updated in this repository.

## Contact
For questions, open an issue in the repository.
